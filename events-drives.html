Current Campaigns

What we're actively working on.

Active — Shipping November

1,000 Care Kits for Dzaleka

In partnership with Carewin Africa Women and Children Foundation, we're assembling Women's Dignity, Baby & Maternal Care, and Children's Education & Comfort kits for families in and around Dzaleka Refugee Camp, Malawi.

Learn More → Give Supplies
Ongoing

Supply Drive for City Rescue Mission

We're continuously collecting food, hygiene items, new clothing, and household essentials for City Rescue Mission, serving families and individuals experiencing homelessness in Lansing.

Learn More → Give Supplies
New

Close to Home: A Fresh Start

Collecting cookware, dishes, towels, small appliances, and household essentials for local families moving from a shelter or crisis situation into their first stable home.

Part of TWCF's new direct family assistance program — supplying a Home Start Kit directly to a family we're connected with, on their terms.
