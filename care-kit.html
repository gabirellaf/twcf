Donation Guidelines

What we accept — and what to avoid.

All donated items must be new, factory-sealed, and unexpired. When in doubt, follow the rule: if you wouldn't give it to someone you love, don't donate it.

General Rules — applies to everything:
✓  New and unused only ✓  Factory sealed (not repacked or resealed) ✓  Expiration dates must be 60+ days out ✓  Label assembled kits with the edition name ✓  Place complete kits in a clear bag or small box ✓  Remove price tags if possible (optional)

Please do not donate the following
✗  Used, open, or resealed items of any kind ✗  Prescription or controlled medications ✗  Expired food, medicine, or personal care items ✗  Homemade or DIY food and personal care products ✗  Used clothing, bedding, or towels ✗  Perishable food or items requiring refrigeration ✗  Glass containers of any kind ✗  Supplements, vitamins, or herbal remedies ✗  Strongly scented products (for shelter kits) ✗  Used toys, books, or electronics ✗  Items without clearly visible expiration dates ✗  Loose, unlabeled, or bulk-packaged items

Items that don't meet these guidelines will be responsibly disposed of and cannot be redistributed. When in doubt, reach out before donating — we're happy to help.
