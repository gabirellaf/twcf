Accepted Items

What we're looking for.

All items must be new, unopened, and not expired. We do not accept medications, opened products, or used clothing.

Hygiene

Shampoo, conditioner, soap, deodorant, toothpaste, toothbrushes, menstrual pads, lotion, face wash

Baby & Infant

Diapers (all sizes), unscented wipes, baby wash, lotion, onesies & sleepers (new), receiving blankets, pacifiers — all new and sealed

School Supplies

Backpacks (new), notebooks, pencils, crayons, markers, folders, glue sticks, scissors, rulers

Clothing

New, packaged only — socks and underwear for women and children of all sizes

Books & Comfort Items

New children's books, stuffed animals (new), puzzles, coloring books, activity books

Non-Perishable Food

Granola bars, crackers, peanut butter, canned goods, cereal — all sealed and not expired

Not sure if your item is accepted? Email us at hello@thewomenandchildrenfoundation.org before shipping.

Donation Handling

How your donation is handled.

✓

Every item is counted, logged, and inspected. Items that are opened, expired, or unapproved are not included in kits.

✓

We do not include medications or medical supplies of any kind in our kits.

✓

Kits are only shipped once logistics are confirmed with a verified recipient organization.

✓

We accept both supplies and monetary donations (Michigan Charitable Solicitation Registration #71710). We are a recognized 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, so monetary gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
