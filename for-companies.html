Partner With TWCF

For Companies

We work with manufacturers, retailers, logistics providers, and corporate giving programs to place useful products directly into the hands of women and children experiencing hardship — locally in Michigan, and internationally with Carewin Africa at Dzaleka Refugee Camp, Malawi.

Ways to Partner

Choose the partnership that fits your business.

Donate Products

Provide new inventory, overstock, or samples that match one of our current needs — see the needs board below.

Sponsor an Event

Put your company behind one of our current campaigns or seasonal drives — see what's coming up on our Events & Drives page.

Sponsor Shipping & Logistics

International shipping is one of our biggest costs. Logistics and freight partners can help us get kits from Michigan to Malawi.

See the Dzaleka Shipping Fund →

Employee Volunteer Opportunities

Bring your team together for a hands-on kit assembly day, or make care kit assembly your next volunteer or team-building event.

Financial Sponsorship

Corporate giving and matching-gift programs can sponsor kit production directly. We are a recognized 501(c)(3), so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Full Project Information

Need more detail for an internal proposal or giving committee? Contact us and we'll send full project information and current product needs.
