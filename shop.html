Where Your Donations Go

Our Partner Organizations

Items collected during this drive are distributed directly to confirmed partner organizations serving women, children, and families in need.

Partner Organization · Lansing, MI

City Rescue Mission of Lansing

Emergency shelter & comprehensive support services

Most needed right now:

  • Non-perishable food: canned vegetables, breakfast cereal, canned fruit, parboiled rice, instant mashed potatoes
  • Hygiene: shampoo, body wash, 3-in-1, men's deodorant
  • New underwear (men & women), pajama pants or shorts
  • Kleenex, laundry pods
Please avoid sending: Blankets, towels, sheets, toilet paper, paper towel, formula, or cleaning supplies — they have commercial agreements for these.
Partner Organization · East Lansing, MI

Haven House

Family shelter & support for women and children

Most needed right now:

  • Hygiene: toothpaste, deodorant (men's & women's), body wash, body lotion, face wash, period products
  • Kids: body wash & shampoo
  • Diapers (sizes 3–7) and pull-ups (all sizes)
  • Baby care: wash, lotion, diaper rash cream, swaddles, nail care kits
  • Household: bath & kitchen towels, cutting boards, silverware, baking sets (gently used OK)
Please avoid sending: Clothing — guests receive thrift store vouchers instead.
