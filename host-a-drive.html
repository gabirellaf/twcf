How It Works

Running a collection is simple.

1

Apply to Partner

Fill out the form below. We'll reach out within a few days to confirm details and answer your questions.

2

We Send Materials

We'll provide a donation list, promotional materials, and everything your team needs to run a successful collection.

3

You Collect

Set up a collection point at your location and gather approved supplies from your community, congregation, students, or colleagues.

4

Ship to Us

Ship the collected items to our address. We handle inspection, assembly, and delivery — you get a report on how the donations were used.

Who Can Partner

We work with organizations of all sizes.

Whether you're a small business, a school classroom, a large church, or a community group — you can run a collection drive. There's no minimum size requirement.

Schools & Universities

Classroom, grade-level, or school-wide campaigns.

Churches & Faith Communities

Congregation drives aligned with your mission of service.

Businesses & Workplaces

Employee giving campaigns and corporate social responsibility.

Community & Civic Groups

Clubs, neighborhood associations, sororities, fraternities.

International Groups

Anyone, anywhere in the world can run a collection and ship to us.
