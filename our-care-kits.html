Our Care Kits

Kit 1

Women's Dignity Kit

Designed for adult women facing hardship, displacement, or crisis. This kit supports personal dignity, hygiene, and comfort during a difficult period.

Best for: shelters, domestic violence organizations, transitional housing programs, refugee support services.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $35

Kit Contents

  • Body wash or 3-in-1 (shampoo/conditioner/body wash)
  • Shampoo
  • Women's deodorant
  • Face wash
  • Body lotion
  • Period products — menstrual pads (1 pack)
  • Underwear — new, packaged
Kit 2

Baby & Maternal Care Kit

For mothers and infants — especially those in situations of instability or postpartum need. Covers practical hygiene and care for both mother and baby.

Best for: family shelters, maternal health programs, postpartum support organizations, refugee family services.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $50

Kit Contents

  • Baby wash, shampoo, and lotion set
  • Diaper rash cream
  • Diapers — sizes 3, 4, 5, 6, or 7 (1 pack)
  • Pull-ups — any size (1 pack)
  • Swaddle blanket (new)
  • Baby nail care kit
Kit 3

Children's Education & Comfort Kit

For school-age children — designed to support education, hygiene, and emotional comfort. Helps children feel prepared and cared for even in unstable situations.

Best for: schools, family shelters, child welfare organizations, refugee camp programs serving children.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $35

Kit Contents

  • Backpack (new)
  • Notebooks and pencils
  • Kid's body wash and shampoo
  • Toothbrush and toothpaste (child-size)
  • Age-appropriate book (new)
  • Small comfort item (stuffed animal, puzzle, or coloring book)
Kit 4

Family Essentials Kit

For families experiencing displacement, homelessness, or crisis. Covers the most essential hygiene and household needs — designed to help a family get through their first difficult days.

Best for: family shelters, transitional housing, disaster relief support, refugee resettlement organizations.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $30

Kit Contents

  • Canned vegetables, canned fruit, or breakfast cereal
  • Parboiled rice or instant mashed potatoes
  • Laundry pods
  • Kleenex / facial tissues
  • Men's deodorant
  • Body wash
  • Men's or women's underwear — new, packaged (1 pair)
Kit 5

Teen Girls' Essentials Kit

Designed for teen girls facing hardship, displacement, or family crisis — filling the gap between our Baby & Maternal Care and Women's Dignity kits with age-appropriate hygiene and comfort items.

Best for: family shelters with teen residents, school counselors, foster-care transition programs.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $45

Kit Contents

  • Teen-formula deodorant
  • Body wash and face wash for acne-prone skin
  • Menstrual pads and tampons (multi-pack)
  • New bra — packaged, various sizes
  • Hairbrush and hair ties
  • Underwear — new, packaged
  • Journal and pen
Kit 6

Hospital Comfort Kit for Kids

For children spending time in a hospital or pediatric care setting. Designed to bring a little comfort and distraction during a stressful stay.

Best for: pediatric hospital wards, children's hospital child-life programs, and our Hospital Toy Drive.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $20

Kit Contents

  • New stuffed animal or small plush toy
  • Coloring book and crayons or markers
  • Small puzzle or activity book
  • Get-well card
  • Age-appropriate book (new)
Kit 7

Winter Warmth Kit

Practical cold-weather items for anyone facing a Michigan winter without reliable shelter or a warm coat.

Best for: shelters, warming centers, and families facing housing instability during winter months.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $30

Kit Contents

  • New winter hat
  • Gloves or mittens (new pair)
  • Thick wool or thermal socks (new, multiple pairs)
  • Hand warmers
  • New thermal base-layer top or bottom
Kit 8

Period Poverty Kit

A smaller, focused kit that addresses period poverty directly — an easy, low-cost way to give when a full Women's Dignity Kit isn't the right fit.

Best for: schools, food pantries, and community organizations distributing period products directly.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $30

Kit Contents

  • Menstrual pads (2 packs)
  • Tampons (1 pack)
  • Panty liners
  • Individual wet wipes
  • Underwear — new, packaged (1 pair)
Kit 9

Home Start Kit

For a family moving from a shelter or crisis situation into their first stable home — often starting with next to nothing in the kitchen. Part of our Close to Home direct family assistance program.

Best for: families transitioning out of a shelter, transitional housing programs, and households rebuilding after housing instability.

Donate Items for This Kit Sponsor for $100

Kit Contents

  • Pots and a frying pan
  • Baking sheet
  • Cooking utensils and a can opener
  • Cutting board and knives
  • Plates, bowls, cups, and silverware
  • Dish towels
  • Food-storage containers
  • Dish soap and sponges
Kit 10

Snack Kit

Individually packaged, shelf-stable snacks for kids and families facing food insecurity — part of our online food pantry, for backpacks, after-school programs, and quick, no-questions-asked requests.

Best for: schools, after-school programs, and families requesting a quick supply through Get Help.

Donate Items for This Kit

Kit Contents

  • Granola or cereal bars (multi-pack)
  • Individual bags of crackers or pretzels
  • Fruit snacks or applesauce pouches
  • Individually wrapped snacks (check for allergies)
  • Juice boxes or shelf-stable drink pouches
  • Peanut-butter or cheese cracker packs
