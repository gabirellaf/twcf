What to Bring

Everything Helps

We accept new, factory-sealed items only. No glass, no prescriptions, no homemade items.

Hygiene & Personal Care

Shampoo, conditioner, soap, deodorant, toothpaste, toothbrush, feminine hygiene products, lotion, wipes

Baby & Infant

Diapers (all sizes), baby wipes, formula, baby food pouches, new onesies & sleepers, receiving blankets, pacifiers, baby bottles

Shelf-Stable Food

Granola bars, nut butter packets, crackers, trail mix, fruit pouches, tuna pouches, oatmeal packets, soup cups

School Supplies

Notebooks, pencils, crayons, markers, folders, glue sticks, scissors, rulers, backpacks (new only)

Clothing

New, packaged socks, underwear, sleepwear — for women and children of all sizes. No used clothing.

First Aid

Sealed bandages, gauze, antiseptic wipes, antibiotic ointment, pain relievers, digital thermometer
