Youth & Student Volunteers

Connecting Young People to Global Service

The Women and Children Foundation's care-kit initiatives are designed not only to deliver needed supplies, but also to introduce young people in the United States to meaningful humanitarian service. Student and youth volunteers help collect, sort, organize, and assemble care kits before they are prepared for delivery to our international partners. By participating directly, volunteers can see how individual contributions become tangible assistance for women and children facing hardship around the world.
