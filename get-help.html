Get Help

Alongside our care kit drives, TWCF is building a direct family assistance program — reaching women, children, and families in Greater Lansing and surrounding communities who are facing financial hardship, housing instability, displacement, crisis, or an inability to obtain basic necessities. If that's you, or a family you know, you can ask us directly.

This is not a crisis line. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For a directory of local emergency and support resources, visit our Greater Lansing Help Directory.
How It Works

A direct conversation, not a long application.

1

Reach Out

Fill out the short form below yourself, or have a shelter, school, church, or caseworker send us your information with your permission.

2

We Talk

We'll follow up to understand what's actually needed — no long intake process, and never more detail than necessary.

3

We Put It Together

Based on what's needed and what we have available, we assemble the right assistance — often one of our care kits, sometimes something more specific.

4

We Connect, On Your Terms

You choose a safe, neutral place to receive assistance. We never require photos or your story as a condition of help.

Who Qualifies

Our service area & eligibility

We serve women, children, and families in Greater Lansing and surrounding communities experiencing financial hardship, housing instability, displacement, crisis, or an inability to obtain basic necessities. Assistance decisions are based on need, using consistent criteria applied to everyone who reaches out — not on personal connections.

Your Privacy

How we keep this safe

  • ✓ Your information is kept confidential and used only to arrange your assistance.
  • ✓ You choose a safe, neutral pickup or meeting location — we don't require your home address.
  • ✓ In-person visits are staffed by at least two TWCF team members, for everyone's safety.
  • ✓ Receiving assistance is never contingent on being photographed, interviewed, or sharing your story.
  • ✓ Children are never interviewed or photographed without appropriate consent.
