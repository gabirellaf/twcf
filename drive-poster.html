Donation Drive

The Comfort
& Care Drive

Collecting essential supplies for women, children, and families in the greater Lansing area who are in need. Every item you donate goes directly to someone who needs it.

June 21 – July 5, 2026
What We're Collecting
Hygiene
  • Toothbrush & toothpaste
  • Shampoo & conditioner
  • Body wash or bar soap
  • Deodorant
  • Wipes & lotion
  • Hair ties, combs, brushes
  • Feminine hygiene products
  • Hand sanitizer
Food — Shelf-Stable Only
  • Granola & protein bars
  • Crackers & rice cakes
  • Nut butter packets
  • Trail mix & dried fruit
  • Fruit pouches & applesauce
  • Tuna / salmon pouches
  • Oatmeal packets
  • Soup cups (pull-tab)
School Supplies
  • Notebooks & folders
  • Pencils, pens, erasers
  • Crayons & colored pencils
  • Markers (washable)
  • Glue sticks & scissors
  • Backpacks (new only)
  • Rulers & pencil cases
Children's & Infant Items
  • Stuffed animals (new only)
  • Coloring & activity books
  • Children's books
  • Small toys (sealed)
  • Diapers (all sizes welcome)
  • Baby wipes (unscented)
  • Baby formula & food pouches
  • Pacifiers & baby bottles (new)
First Aid
  • Bandages (assorted, sealed)
  • Gauze & medical tape
  • Antiseptic wipes
  • Antibiotic ointment
  • Ibuprofen / acetaminophen
  • Digital thermometer
Clothing & Other
  • Socks — new & packaged
  • Underwear — new & packaged
  • Baby onesies & sleepers (new)
  • Receiving blankets (new)
  • Encouragement cards
  • Small journals & pens
  • Laundry detergent pods
✓ All items must be new & unused
✓ Factory sealed — never repacked
✓ Check expiration dates
✗ No glass · No prescriptions · No homemade items
How to Donate
1

Choose any items from the list above. New and sealed only.
2
