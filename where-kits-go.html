Distribution

Where Kits Go

Every kit we assemble goes to a specific recipient through a trusted partner organization. We do not distribute kits directly to individuals — we work through established organizations that serve women, children, and families.

Who We Serve

Kits reach people facing real hardship.

International Recipients

Women, children, and families in refugee camps and under-resourced communities outside the United States — served through vetted international partner organizations.

Women in Crisis

Women experiencing domestic violence, homelessness, displacement, or health-related hardship — served through shelters and partner organizations.

Mothers & Infants

New and expecting mothers and their infants — particularly those experiencing postpartum hardship, housing instability, or poverty.

Children

School-age children in under-resourced households, families in shelters, or communities with limited access to school supplies and hygiene products.

Families in Displacement

Families experiencing homelessness, housing instability, refugee resettlement, or crisis displacement who need immediate essential supplies.

Current Initiatives

Where we're working now.

Around the World

Carewin Africa Women and Children Foundation

Carewin Africa Women and Children Foundation serves vulnerable women, children, and families in and around Dzaleka Refugee Camp in Malawi — one of the longest-running refugee camps in Africa — including families displaced by conflict.

Their needs include food assistance, hygiene and menstrual-care supplies, baby and maternal-care items, clothing, school supplies, and support for vulnerable children.

Status: Active partnership. We are currently coordinating supply needs and shipping logistics with Carewin. Kit types: Women's Dignity Kits, Baby & Maternal Care Kits, Children's Education & Comfort Kits.

Goal: 1,000 care kits for displaced families by November 2026.

Close to Home

City Rescue Mission — Lansing, MI

City Rescue Mission serves families and individuals experiencing homelessness and housing instability in Lansing. We are actively collecting supplies for delivery to their program.

What they need most:
  • Non-perishable food (canned vegetables, cereal, canned fruit, rice, instant mashed potatoes)
  • Personal hygiene items (shampoo, body wash, 3-in-1, men's deodorant, toothpaste)
  • New packaged clothing (men's & women's underwear, pajama pants or shorts)
  • Household items (Kleenex, laundry pods)
Please note — they do not need: blankets, towels, sheets, toilet paper, paper towels, formula, or standard cleaning supplies.

Our Standards

How we choose distribution partners.

✓

We contact every recipient organization before designing kits — kits are built around their stated needs, not our assumptions.

✓

Distribution requirements and logistics are fully confirmed before any kits are shipped or delivered.

✓

We do not publish the names of partner organizations until formal agreements are in place and permission has been given.
