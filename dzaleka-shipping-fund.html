Why a Shipping Fund?

Donated supplies still have to make the journey.

Product donations solve only one part of a humanitarian shipment. Supplies must still be packed, transported, cleared through customs, and delivered to the receiving community. TWCF is fundraising for this transport chain so our partner in Malawi is not asked to pay any freight or arrival charges.

What the fund covers

  • Michigan collection and transport to the departure point
  • International freight and documentation
  • Malawi customs/terminal handling and applicable duties or taxes
  • Final delivery to Dzaleka
Transparency note

Recent preliminary door-to-door freight estimates were approximately $18,000–$19,500 before certain variable charges. The final amount may change depending on shipment dimensions, customs treatment, and final routing. We will update this page as those details are confirmed.

FAQ

Questions about the shipping fund

Why do donated supplies need financial support?

Even donated items must be transported, documented, cleared, and delivered. These are expenses that product donations alone cannot cover.

Who prepares the kits?

TWCF volunteers prepare the kits in Michigan before shipment.

Who will receive and distribute them?

Carewin Africa Women and Children Foundation, our project partner serving families in Dzaleka Refugee Camp, Malawi, is the planned receiving and distribution partner.

Can Carewin pay customs or delivery charges?

No. Our shipment will proceed only under arrangements in which these costs are provided for without passing charges to the receiving partner.

Is $20,000 the final transportation cost?

No. It is a preliminary fundraising goal based on current estimates. Final charges and any applicable taxes are still being confirmed, and the target may be adjusted transparently.

When will the kits arrive?

We are preparing the shipment for the coming months. Timing depends on completing kit assembly, securing adequate funding, confirming customs arrangements, and booking appropriate freight. We will publish updates when the timeline is confirmed.

What if the amount raised is not sufficient or the shipment changes?

Funds designated for this campaign will remain allocated to the stated Dzaleka relief logistics purpose while we pursue feasible shipment arrangements. If plans materially change, TWCF will provide an update and handle restricted gifts in accordance with donor restrictions and applicable requirements.

Can I donate items instead?

Yes. To discuss an in-kind contribution, email hello@thewomenandchildrenfoundation.org with the items and approximate quantity available. This specific fundraiser focuses on transportation costs.
