How It Works

A system built around real need.

This is what distinguishes TWCF from a general donation drive — every step is deliberate.

1

We Listen

Recipient organizations tell us what the people they serve actually need — before we assemble a single kit.

2

We Collect

Individuals, businesses, schools, and community groups contribute approved supplies — in person, by mail, or through organized collection drives.

3

We Assemble

Volunteers organize supplies into complete, purpose-built care kits — every item counted and inspected before it goes in.

4

We Deliver

Kits are distributed through trusted community organizations — shelters, family support services, and partner groups — locally and internationally.

Around the World

Carewin Africa Women and Children Foundation

Carewin Africa serves vulnerable women, children, and families in and around Dzaleka Refugee Camp in Malawi. Their needs include food assistance, hygiene and menstrual-care supplies, baby and maternal-care items, clothing, school supplies, and support for vulnerable children. Our Standards

How we handle every donation.

✓

Every recipient organization is contacted before kits are designed — we build around real, stated needs.

✓

Every donated item is counted and inspected. We do not include medications, opened products, expired products, or unapproved items.

✓

Distribution requirements are confirmed with recipient organizations before kits are shipped or delivered.

✓

We are registered with the State of Michigan to solicit charitable donations (Registration #71710) and recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Monetary gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
