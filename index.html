What We Do

Relief, Connection, and Hope.

Real, visible impact — built to reach every woman and child who needs us, no matter where you are.

Care Kits

Hygiene products, food, school supplies, and essentials — assembled and distributed through trusted partner organizations.

See Kit Editions
Community Partnerships

We work with shelters, domestic violence organizations, and healthcare providers to reach people safely and responsibly.

Education & Outreach

Building awareness around domestic violence, sexual assault, and women's health — and connecting people with trusted, accredited resources.

How It Works

From you to someone in need.

Every action — big or small — moves directly through our foundation to reach women and children.

1

Donate or Join

Donate supplies, become a member, or sign up to volunteer — every contribution fuels the work.

2

We Assemble

Volunteers and staff pack care kits with hygiene products, food, and school supplies — ready to go to people who need them.

3

It Reaches Them

Care kits are distributed through partner organizations — shelters, hospitals, schools, and community centers — to women and children in need.
